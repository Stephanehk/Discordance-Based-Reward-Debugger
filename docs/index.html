Discordance-Based Reward Debugger

A tool for finding misspecifications in reward functions.

Input: a reward function implemented in Python and your environment's source code.
Output: comparisons where our tool hypothesizes your preferences will most likely differ from the ranking induced by the reward function.

In light of those hypotheses, you update your reward function as you see fit.

Our results: In popular RL environments such as Meta-World, our discordance-based reward debugger uncovers previously documented and novel reward misspecifications. In ExploitGym, it uncovers a reward misspecification that was later exploited in practice, when a collective of AI agents attacked OpenAI and Hugging Face infrastructure while being evaluated on similar tasks.
