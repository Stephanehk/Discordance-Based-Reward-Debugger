difficult — don't default to a fixed number. Compute the designed-
reward indifference point p* = (return(C) - return(B)) /
(return(A) - return(B)), then move slightly off it (usually onto the
C-preferring side) so the reward gives a small-but-real preference
that a human might intuitively reverse. The exact (1-p) depends on
the reward magnitudes in the picked trajectories — sometimes very
small, sometimes not. Justify the chosen p in the selection file.
If picking two lotteries, prefer two different shapes (different
C/A/B or a different trade-off) over two values of p on the same
shape — sweeping p on one shape is a single question rendered twice.
